# Business Validation Checklist

These items should be confirmed with Street Bowl Café during/after the next demo.

## Tax and invoicing

- [ ] Is Street Bowl Café VAT-registered or non-VAT?
- [ ] What exact registered business name, trade name, TIN, registered address, and branch code must appear on invoices?
- [ ] What invoice numbering / BIR POS registration requirements apply to the replacement system?
- [ ] Are prices tax-inclusive?
- [ ] Confirm Senior Citizen and PWD calculation rules used by the business.
- [ ] Confirm whether promotional/employee/student discounts exist.

## Payments

- [ ] Confirm all accepted methods: Cash, GCash, Maya, cards, bank transfer, others.
- [ ] Does the café need split payment on one order?
- [ ] Is online payment reference verification required?
- [ ] Who may authorize refunds?

## Shifts

- [ ] Must cashiers enter opening cash?
- [ ] Must cashiers count closing cash?
- [ ] Does management need over/short variance?
- [ ] Can managers process orders without an active cashier shift?
- [ ] Is shift handoff between employees allowed?

## Menu

- [ ] Confirm actual menu categories.
- [ ] Confirm product variants and prices.
- [ ] Confirm modifier/customization groups.
- [ ] Confirm which modifiers change price.
- [ ] Confirm which modifiers consume extra ingredients.

## Inventory

- [ ] Confirm all tracked raw ingredients.
- [ ] Confirm base units for each ingredient.
- [ ] Confirm packaging materials that should be tracked.
- [ ] Confirm finished goods that should deduct as one piece.
- [ ] Confirm recipe quantity per menu variant.
- [ ] Confirm low-stock/reorder thresholds.
- [ ] Confirm expiry-tracked items.
- [ ] Confirm whether negative stock is ever allowed.

## Purchasing

- [ ] Does management regularly create purchase orders or mostly buy directly?
- [ ] Do suppliers allow credit terms?
- [ ] What supplier invoice/receipt information is available?
- [ ] Who may approve purchases?
- [ ] Who may receive deliveries?

## Waste and stock corrections

- [ ] Confirm reason categories: spoiled, damaged, expired, spill, complimentary, staff meal, count correction.
- [ ] Who may record waste?
- [ ] Which movements require manager approval?

## Refunds

- [ ] Confirm full refund policy.
- [ ] Confirm partial refund policy.
- [ ] Confirm whether refunded sealed products can ever return to stock.

## Device and operations

- [ ] Confirm Android tablet model/OS.
- [ ] Confirm thermal printer model and 58mm/80mm paper width.
- [ ] Confirm whether operations must continue during internet outages.
- [ ] Confirm expected number of simultaneous POS devices.
